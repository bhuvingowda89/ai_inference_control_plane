# Graphical abstract specification

**Status:** design only; whether FGCS requires or accepts it remains unverified because its guide returned 403.

Create an original vector schematic, left to right: candidate revision and progressive traffic; system and behavior evidence tagged with revision/epoch; desired, observed, and effective state; SafeRoll; three possible outputs PROMOTE, HOLD, ROLLBACK. A thin bottom annotation should say ‘safer immediate promotion decisions; measurable HOLD/progress cost’ without embedding selective numeric results. No borrowed icons or figures. Do not use a general-purpose generative image tool for the graphical abstract under [Elsevier's AI policy](https://www.elsevier.com/en-au/about/policies-and-standards/generative-ai-policies-for-journals).

[Elsevier's general graphical-abstract guidance](https://www.elsevier.com/researcher/author/tools-and-resources/graphical-abstract) specifies a separate file, minimum 1328 × 531 pixels, at least 300 dpi, approximately 500:200 ratio, preferred TIFF/EPS/PDF/MS Office, large readable type, and no separate heading or synopsis. Recheck against FGCS guide before production.
