# Novelty statement

Among the inspected MLOps, serving, and canary-deployment sources, SafeRoll studies a specific decision contract: bind operational and model-behavior evidence to rollout revision, epoch, and controller-visible serving state before promotion, HOLD, or rollback. Existing infrastructure may be extensible to implement pieces of this contract. The paper evaluates the contract and its safety costs; it does not claim to be the first or only possible implementation.
