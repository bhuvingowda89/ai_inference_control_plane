# Editorial fit

[FGCS's official scope](https://shop.elsevier.com/journals/future-generation-computer-systems/0167-739X) covers computing infrastructure, distributed systems, cloud platforms, and infrastructure development and monitoring. SafeRoll addresses the control plane of continuously operated AI inference services. Its unit of decision is a progressively exposed serving revision, with operational signals, model behavior, revision/epoch attribution, observed routing, and effective traffic as runtime inputs. This places the work in cloud-native AI infrastructure, MLOps orchestration, model-serving reliability, and recovery. The 650-run frozen comparison and six pinned CPU runs evaluate deployment control rather than a new model algorithm or a generic testing tool. The central gap is whether evidence used to promote a revision actually belongs to that revision and to the serving state being decided upon. The manuscript also reports the cost of state-aware abstention and the gap between rollback request and physical containment.
